# Code of Conduct
 
We want everyone who works on AgenTrust Develop for Good to feel welcome, respected and safe. This applies in the repo, in pull requests and issues, in Slack, and in meetings.
 
## What we expect
 
- **Be kind and respectful.** Assume people are trying to help.
- **Give feedback on the work, not the person.** Say what to change and why.
- **Ask questions freely.** Nobody has to pretend they already know something.
- **Share credit.** Name the people who helped you.
- **Respect different backgrounds, time zones and levels of experience.** Not everyone can join every meeting. Written updates are always welcome.
- **Keep private information private.** Do not share personal data, passwords or keys.
## What is not acceptable
 
- Harassment, insults, threats or personal attacks
- Discrimination of any kind, including jokes about someone's background, identity or ability
- Unwelcome attention or messages after someone has asked you to stop
- Sharing someone's private information without their permission
- Disrupting discussions on purpose
## If something goes wrong

The project team may remove comments, close issues or pull requests, or remove someone from the project when the code is broken.
 
## Scope
 
This code covers all project spaces and anyone representing the project in public.
